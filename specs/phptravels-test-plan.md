# PHPTRAVELS Website QA Test Plan

## Application Overview

Comprehensive functional, negative, boundary, accessibility, responsive, security, and integration coverage for `https://phptravels.net/`. Use fresh contexts and controlled mocks for suppliers, timeouts, empty results, and session expiry.

## Test Areas

1. Public home page and demo notice
2. Service tabs, menus, currency, and language controls
3. Stay search, validation, results, filters, property details, and booking continuity
4. Flight, visa, car rental, and AI Trip Planner entry points
5. Login, registration, account lifecycle, and protected-content access
6. Demo booking safety and controlled failure handling
7. Footer, legal, support, contact, and external links
8. Keyboard accessibility, responsive layout, HTTPS, and sensitive-data handling

Environment-dependent flows require approved non-production credentials or controlled fixtures. Real payment data and production reservations are out of scope.
